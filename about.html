About Unbound Devices

A phone experience
that puts you first.

Unbound Devices is for people who want to make more deliberate choices about the technology they carry every day.

Why Unbound Devices

Your phone should work for you.

Phones are part of everyday life, but the experience should still feel personal. Unbound Devices brings together phones and a privacy-minded operating system for people looking for a different approach. Google and Apple shouldn't track you. Make the switch and turn on privacy.

We aim to make it easier to explore that choice, understand what comes with a device, and decide whether it fits the way you want to use your phone.

Privacy first

No Google apps by default, only the essentials needed to get you going on your privacy journey.

Still get it done

Based on the Google Pixel phones and GrapheneOS, you still have a current phone that can do everything a "regular" phone can, but without being tracked.
