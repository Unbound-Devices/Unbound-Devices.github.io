Privacy and security

GrapheneOS includes privacy and security improvements to Android, including stronger app sandboxing and additional controls.

✳

Your choice of apps

Google apps and services are never included by default but can be installed through the GrapheneOS App Store if needed.

⌁

Android app compatibility

This isn't some alien phone that cuts you off from the world. 90% of Android apps work without Google Play Services and can be installed through Aurora Store (pre-installed) and those that don't work can be used from the offical Google Play Store.

◇

Your phone, your setup

Set up your device with the apps and preferences that work for you. No more useless bloatware, just the apps you want. Plus enjoy a few essential apps installed to get you started.

The operating system

Meet GrapheneOS

GrapheneOS is an open source Android OS prioritizing privacy and security. Unbound Devices is based on GrapheneOS, but not affiliated with them.
