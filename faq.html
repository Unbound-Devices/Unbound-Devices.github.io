Orders & shipping

Where do you ship to?

We currently ship within Canada only.

How do you ship?

All items are shipped via Canada Post.

What currency are the prices in?

Prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and may change due to changes in supply prices. Please contact us to confirm the current price and availability before ordering.

What payment methods do you accept?

Currently, we accept e-transfer only.

Is a deposit required?

Yes, a 50% deposit is required for all orders.

How do I place an order?

Email us at unbounddevices@duck.com with the Pixel model you’re interested in. We’ll confirm current availability, price, and the next steps.

How much does shipping cost, and how long will it take?

Shipping costs and delivery estimates can depend on the order and destination. Contact us with your location and the device you’re interested in so we can discuss the details.

Devices & software

What phones are available?

See the devices page for the listed Google Pixel models. Availability may vary, so contact us to confirm before ordering.

What is GrapheneOS?

GrapheneOS is an independent, open-source mobile operating system focused on privacy and security. It is based on Android and supports Android apps.

Do Unbound Devices phones use GrapheneOS?

Unbound Devices phones are based on GrapheneOS. Visit our About page to learn more.

Can I use Android apps?

Yes. Android apps are fully compatible on your phone.

Can I ask for help setting up my device?

Yes. Contact us with your device model and a short description of what you need help with.

Do you accept returns?

Yes, but read the Returns Page for full terms and conditions.
