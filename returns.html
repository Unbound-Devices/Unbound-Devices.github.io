How to get help

Start with an email.

  1. 01

    Contact us

    Email devdude437@protonmail.com before returning or shipping a device.

  2. 02

    Share the relevant details

    Include your order information, the device model, and a brief description of the issue or reason for your request.

  3. 03

    Wait for instructions

    We’ll reply to discuss your request and confirm any applicable return, refund, or exchange details before you send anything.

Please contact us first.

Return eligibility, timing, and available resolutions depend on the circumstances of the order. Don’t mail a device until you’ve received instructions from us. A device may be returned within 30 days of receiving it but it must be in its original, undamaged packaging and have all included accessories that came in the box. You are responsible to pay shipping fees to ship it back. A refund will not be issued until the device is received by us and we approve it for return. If we deny the return, no refund will be issued and the device won't be sent back. Please contact us for full details.
